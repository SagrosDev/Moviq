- (decision by user) Moviqo is a bilingual multi-tenant SaaS platform for SMEs to create and execute operational workflows without technical staff.
- (decision by user) MVP supports forms, tasks, assignments, decisions, attachments, configurable states, publishing, execution, audit history, and task-assignment notifications.
- (decision by user) Initial customer remains a broad SME hypothesis; no pilot industry or company has been selected.
- (decision by user) Moviqo will begin with a free beta and organization-based pricing remains a later validation decision.
- (decision by user) Free infrastructure is acceptable for beta validation, with no formal uptime or recovery guarantee and restrictions on sensitive data.
- (decision by user) Published workflow versions with history cannot be modified or deleted; active-instance migration rules require later system design.
- (gap by user) Need define acceptable beta data, security and recovery threshold, notification channels, migration behavior, and evidence for paid conversion.
- (decision by user) Product name confirmed as Moviqo.
- (decision by user) Minimum workflow elements are Start, Task, Transition, End, user/team assignment, and conditional routing; task forms and calculations support data capture.
- (decision by user) Beta targets: 5 organizations, 20 published workflows, 100 completed process instances, and at least one month of observed use.
- (decision by user) Compatible workflow revisions propagate to active instances; active/completed tasks and history cannot be deleted or rewritten, and incompatible publication is blocked.
- (decision by user) Designers control workflow data and are responsible for lawful collection; Moviqo remains responsible for platform safeguards and will warn against unsupported sensitive data.
- (decision by user) Email assignment notifications are MVP; WhatsApp is a high-value stretch capability subject to budget and operational feasibility.
- (decision by user) Zero-budget beta still requires off-site database exports, separate attachment backup/export, and a tested restoration procedure before onboarding customers.
- (gap by user) Need define day-30 retention threshold, detailed active-instance compatibility rules, backup destination/frequency, and WhatsApp feasibility.
- (event by user) Product brief updated with confirmed MVP scope, beta targets, data responsibility, notification direction, and zero-cost backup release gates.
- (decision by user) Beta retention target confirmed: at least 2 of the 5 organizations remain active at day 30.
- (decision by user) WhatsApp notifications are deferred until after initial beta validation; email remains the MVP assignment-notification channel.
- (gap by coach) User proposed weekly backups; frequency remains open because weekly recovery permits up to 7 days of customer-data loss.
- (decision by user) Zero-cost beta backup policy confirmed: daily logical database export, daily export of new attachments, encrypted off-site storage, 7 daily and 4 weekly copies retained, and monthly restoration testing.
- (gap by coach) Need select the off-site destination for encrypted beta backups during architecture planning.
- (decision by user) Active process instances remain at their current execution point and use the newest published workflow revision for future routing; they never replay or migrate backward.
- (decision by user) An element with an open task cannot be deactivated; completed task instances and submissions remain immutable.
- (decision by user) Retired task definitions are hidden from the active canvas and retained in an Inactive Elements section for history.
- (decision by user) Field identifiers and types are immutable; removing a field from a form preserves captured values, and referenced fields cannot be retired until dependencies are removed.
- (decision by user) Workflow publication validates active-task continuity, valid references and assignments, and that every reachable path can eventually reach an active End element.
- (gap by user) Need define runtime fallback when an assignee becomes inactive after publication and whether retired elements may be reactivated.
- (decision by user) New validation in a previously completed task does not reopen it automatically; the designer may add a condition that routes the active instance backward when required data is empty.
- (decision by user) Backward transitions and loops are supported, and corrective routing is the workflow designer responsibility.
- (decision by user) A never-executed and unreferenced task definition may be deleted permanently; a task with historical instances can only be retired to the Inactive Elements section.
- (decision by user) Publishing validates resolvable references and assignments and confirms an active Start can reach an active End without unintended reachable dead ends; cycles are allowed.
- (decision by user) Open tasks assigned to a user or team that later becomes inactive require manual administrator reassignment in the MVP, with attention status and audit history.
- (direction by user) Automatic periodic detection and reassignment of tasks is deferred beyond MVP.
- (decision by user) A retired task may be reactivated with its original stable identity; it returns to the active canvas and the designer chooses its position and connections.
- (decision by user) Workflow graph validation runs when the designer saves and publication is blocked while validation errors remain.
- (gap by coach) Need decide whether validation errors block draft persistence or save the draft with an invalid status while blocking only publication.
- (decision by user) Draft workflows may be saved with validation errors so incomplete work is preserved; publication remains blocked until all blocking errors are resolved.
- (decision by user) All published workflow versions are preserved as immutable history; restoring an older version creates a new draft and publishes the next sequential version rather than moving a version pointer backward.
- (decision by user) Workflow restoration records its source version, designer, timestamp, and reason; it preserves later data/history and must pass active-instance validation before publication.
- (override by user) Supersedes earlier generic Decision wording: Conditional Routing is a distinct workflow object that selects exactly one outgoing route using configured conditions or flow decisions.
- (override by user) Supersedes earlier permanent-deletion wording: only never-published draft tasks may be physically deleted; published tasks remain in immutable historical versions even when never executed.
- (override by user) Supersedes the earlier unresolved migration decision: active instances stay at their current execution point and use the newest published revision for future routing, with open-task and history safeguards.
- (override by user) Supersedes the earlier WhatsApp stretch direction: WhatsApp notifications are deferred beyond initial beta validation; email is the MVP channel.
- (decision by user) Beta workflow metric counts 20 distinct workflow definitions, excluding additional versions of the same workflow.
- (decision by user) A completed beta process instance is a closed instance that successfully reaches the End element.
- (decision by user) Day-30 organization activity requires a login plus product activity such as workflow design, process-data entry, or task completion.
- (decision by user) Time-to-first-workflow starts at Create Workflow and ends at successful publication; maximum target is 60 minutes and desired target is 30 minutes.
- (decision by user) Workflow validation requires exactly one Start, exactly one End, at least one Task, no unreachable active elements or dead ends, and valid Conditional Routing conditions plus a default path; cycles remain allowed.
- (decision by user) Spanish is the default product language; users may select English, system UI and emails follow user language, and designer-authored labels remain as entered in MVP.
- (decision by user) Execution audit records organization, instance, workflow version, stable element ID, user, timestamp, and outcome/path for tasks, submissions, conditions, and transitions.
- (event by user) Expert brief review corrections applied; earlier versioning, notification, retention, validation, deletion, and draft-save gaps are resolved or explicitly superseded.
