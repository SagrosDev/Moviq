- (event) Express distillation started from the finalized Moviqo PRD, UX DESIGN, UX EXPERIENCE, and approved architecture spine.
- (decision) The PRD, UX DESIGN, UX EXPERIENCE, and architecture spine remain adopted companions because downstream implementation needs their load-bearing detail; SPEC.md provides the lean canonical kernel.
- (capability) CAP-1 — Public acquisition and onboarding: visitors can understand the bilingual beta offer, register, verify identity, create one Organization, and activate its initial Owner; success is a complete landing-to-verified-Owner journey with accurate beta, privacy, and prohibited-data disclosures.
- (capability) CAP-2 — Organization identity and administration: Owners and Administrators can manage one-Organization accounts, hierarchical access, users, Teams, settings, ownership continuity, and safe deactivation; success is server-enforced authorized administration with preserved historical identity and no orphaned open work.
- (capability) CAP-3 — Workflow catalog and lifecycle: authorized Designers can create, discover, duplicate, archive, reactivate, version, compare, and restore Organization Workflows; success is an auditable catalog lifecycle with unique active names, immutable publications, and preserved runtime history.
- (capability) CAP-4 — Visual Workflow and Form design: non-technical Designers can configure valid Start, Task, Conditional Routing, Transition, and End graphs plus responsive Task Forms and reusable Process Fields without BPMN or code; success is publication of a representative valid Workflow with actionable correction of invalid drafts.
- (capability) CAP-5 — Deterministic business rules and calculations: Designers can author typed visual conditions, validation, calculations, conditional Form behavior, and ordered routing; success is identical authoritative results for preview and execution, safe fallback behavior, and publication rejection of invalid or circular dependencies.
- (capability) CAP-6 — Process and Task execution: authorized Members can start Processes, claim or open Tasks, save valid progress, complete Tasks, follow routes and loops, and reach End while preserving Process Data; success is an all-or-nothing execution with no duplicate next work and exact version attribution.
- (capability) CAP-7 — Assignment and operational recovery: work can resolve to Members, Teams, initiators, or User References, and administrators can resolve invalid assignments; success is exclusive Team claiming, correct access transfer, a visible Needs Attention state, and complete assignment audit.
- (capability) CAP-8 — Authorized work views and timelines: users can find startable Processes, actionable Tasks, authorized Process progress, and their own contributions while administrators can inspect operational views; success is searchable, filterable, responsive visibility that never reveals another tenant or unauthorized Task data.
- (capability) CAP-9 — Private files and portable exports: authorized users can upload, inspect, preview, download, remove, and audit attachments, and Owners can request temporary Organization exports; success is private capability-based access, enforced file states and limits, revocation after removal, and expiring export delivery.
- (capability) CAP-10 — Notifications and delivery tracking: Designers can opt into assignment and Transition email and Organizations can opt into operational-problem email; success is localized, idempotent delivery tracking with safe content and business commits independent of email failure.
- (capability) CAP-11 — Audit, retention, and Organization lifecycle: authorized users can inspect appropriate immutable history while Owners can export, close, recover, or allow disclosed inactivity lifecycle processing; success is governed, auditable retention and deletion with minimal isolated historical evidence and no recoverable customer workspace after expiry.
- (capability) CAP-12 — Bilingual, regional, responsive, and accessible experience: Moviqo-owned content supports Spanish and English, shared business values follow Organization regional settings, and core operational flows work accessibly across supported devices; success is consistent localization and completion of critical flows by keyboard and assistive-technology users.
- (capability) CAP-13 — Tenant-safe and recoverable platform operation: the service enforces Organization isolation, secure identity and sessions, safe diagnostics, private storage, observable operation, recovery objectives, and evidence-gated releases; success is zero confirmed cross-Organization exposure and recovery within approved objectives.
- (capability) CAP-14 — End-to-end beta delivery: company testers can exercise the complete product with persistent synthetic data before production safeguards authorize real customer data; success is Gate 1 landing-to-completed-Process execution without developer intervention and Gate 2 promotion only after every real-data safeguard passes.
- (constraint) The finalized PRD is product authority; its adopted UX and architecture companions bind implementation detail, and superseded Brief or PADR decisions cannot be reintroduced.
- (constraint) One MVP account belongs to exactly one Organization; Member, Designer, Administrator, and Owner are hierarchical, and every protected operation is denied by default unless active Organization context and authorization agree.
- (constraint) Published Workflow versions, completed Task occurrences, selected routes, and semantic audit history are immutable; active work uses one exact compatible definition per protected operation.
- (constraint) State-changing commands are idempotent and all-or-nothing across business state, audit, idempotency outcome, and outbox evidence; external delivery cannot reverse committed business state.
- (constraint) Rules, calculations, validation, and routing use one typed deterministic visual language; arbitrary scripts, raw expressions, and client-authoritative business semantics are prohibited.
- (constraint) Process Data and private files are exposed only through current server authorization; tenant isolation, authorization, files, exports, jobs, notifications, search, and audit are Organization-scoped.
- (constraint) Moviqo-owned interfaces are Spanish-first with English support; Designer-authored content remains verbatim and shared business values follow the Organization regional format, timezone, and immutable currency identity.
- (constraint) Operational flows support responsive mobile through desktop use; Workflow and Form authoring targets at least 1280 by 720, while critical flows use WCAG 2.2 A/AA as their verification baseline.
- (constraint) Normal Claim, Save, Complete, and standard configuration operations target two-second p95; application views target three-second usability under the documented beta profile of twenty Organizations and fifty concurrent users.
- (constraint) Internal E2E is company-only and synthetic-only; real customer data is forbidden until live file inspection, independent backups and restore evidence, lifecycle enforcement, tenant/security gates, and accessibility evidence pass.
- (constraint) The internal E2E deployment uses the approved Python/Django modular monolith, React SPA, PostgreSQL, Firebase Hosting, Cloud Run, Supabase database hosting, private GCS, and Resend topology defined by the architecture companion.
- (constraint) Delivery follows pragmatic TDD: red-green-refactor, real PostgreSQL integration and tenant-isolation tests, API and architecture contracts, and Playwright critical journeys; failing applicable checks block promotion.
- (constraint) Free-tier allowances are operating ceilings, not product guarantees; monitor capacity at 60, 80, and 90 percent and require a paid upgrade before an active Organization is impaired.
- (non-goal) MVP does not provide BPMN compatibility, parallel routing, subprocesses, timers, reminders, SLAs, arbitrary scripts, general-purpose customer automation, or external API integrations.
- (non-goal) MVP does not provide AI, advanced analytics or process intelligence, real-time collaborative editing, microservices, brokers, or distributed caches.
- (non-goal) MVP does not provide anonymous or public Process initiation, multi-Organization identities, external participants, MFA, SSO, passkeys, or social/passwordless sign-in.
- (non-goal) MVP does not provide round-robin or workload assignment, automatic reassignment, multiple custom Task outcome buttons, WhatsApp, SMS, push notifications, or a configurable notification center.
- (non-goal) MVP does not provide native mobile applications, narrow-screen Workflow authoring, formal uptime commitments, permanent free pricing, or automatic charges.
- (success) Gate 1 succeeds when company testers begin at the deployed landing page and independently register isolated Organizations, design and publish a valid Workflow, execute assignments, rules, files, routes, loops, versions, and notifications, reach End, and inspect persisted history using synthetic data.
- (success) Public beta validates value when at least five Organizations participate, twenty distinct Workflows are published, one hundred Processes complete, two of the first five Organizations remain active on day thirty, one non-technical Owner publishes a simple Workflow within sixty minutes, one Organization completes the full flow without developer intervention, and one Organization records willingness to consider a paid plan.
- (success) Release trust succeeds when no confirmed cross-Organization exposure occurs, no customer-data loss remains unrecovered beyond the 24-hour RPO/RTO, and every applicable Gate 2 control has retained evidence.
