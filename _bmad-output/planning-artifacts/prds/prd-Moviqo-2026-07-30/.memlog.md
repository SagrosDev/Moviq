- (event) PRD create run initialized from the Moviqo Product Brief and its decision log.
- (decision) PRD will define behavioral requirements and acceptance consequences; implementation mechanisms are deferred to UX and architecture.
- (decision by user) PRD uses Fast Path and launch-level rigor for a public beta with real customer-defined business data.
- (decision by user) Complete PRD source set is Product Brief, Product Brief decision log, and user-authored Moviqo PADR.
- (decision by user) PRD confirmed: a user may be physically deleted only when no historical instance, workflow-creation, or audit reference exists; deactivation is blocked until assigned open Tasks are reassigned; Conditional Routing evaluates matching conditions in designer-defined priority order.
- (decision by user) Owners and Administrators may start any published workflow for operational support; Members and Designers require authorization through the workflow's configured starters.
- (decision by user) Approved account model: every Organization user is a Member; MVP access levels inherit Member capabilities as Member, Designer, Administrator, and Owner; Auditor is deferred. Participation grants only limited instance progress and access to one's authorized Tasks and submitted data, not other users' Tasks. Designers do not automatically receive production-data access; Owners and Administrators receive audited organization-wide operational access.
- (decision by user) Dashboard visibility approved: Members and Designers see only Workflows they are authorized to start; Owners and Administrators see all published Workflows. Direct or eligible Team Task assignment exposes only the related instance and its limited status, and does not grant authority to start another instance of that Workflow.
- (decision by user) MVP Team assignment uses exclusive claiming: a Task assigned to a Team is Available to all active Team Members, one Member must claim it before editing or completion, and the claim is audited. Collaborative and other advanced assignment strategies are deferred.
- (decision by user) Team management approved: Owners and Administrators manage Teams and memberships; Members may belong to multiple Teams; Designers may reference valid Teams but cannot administer them without elevated access; historically referenced Teams are deactivated rather than deleted; Team deactivation requires reassignment of open Tasks; empty or inactive Teams are invalid production assignees or starters.
- (decision by user) Approved MVP Task assignment methods: specific active Member, specific active Team, Workflow Initiator, and dynamic User Reference. Invalid or unresolved runtime assignment enters Needs Reassignment, appears in the Owners' and Administrators' instance-management inbox with its failure reason, and requires audited manual reassignment before work can continue.
- (decision by user) Text-field MVP approved: Short Text has length constraints, default/placeholder/help, and friendly preset formats without raw regular expressions; Long Text is multiline plain text and rich text is deferred; calculations use a separate Calculated Field; common conditional Form behavior applies to both.
- (decision by user) Numeric safety requirement: Integer values use explicit platform boundaries and narrower optional Designer constraints; client and server validation must reject invalid or out-of-range values before draft save or Task completion; every server-accepted value must be guaranteed representable by persistence without overflow.
- (decision by user) Numeric-field defaults approved: Integer 0..999,999,999; Decimal and Currency 0.00..999,999,999.99 with two decimal places by default; default data value is empty; negative values are off by default but may be enabled; Designers may change valid limits; numeric display follows locale while stored values remain invariant.
- (decision by user) Date and Date-Time scope approved: empty or fixed/Today/Now defaults; fixed, relative, or field-referenced minimum and maximum rules; supported range 1900-01-01 through 2100-12-31; locale-aware display and Organization timezone for Date-Time; pre-persistence validation; availability to conditions, routing, and date calculations.
- (decision by user) Date-entry UX approved: every Date and Date-Time control offers localized manual entry plus an on-demand calendar picker; Date-Time includes time selection; manual and picker paths enforce identical constraints; appropriate native mobile selectors may be used.
- (decision by user) Yes/No field approved with explicit Yes/No selector as default and optional confirmation-checkbox presentation; initial value is empty unless configured; required selector demands an explicit answer while required confirmation requires checked; values are available to conditions, calculations, and routing.
- (decision by user) Choice-list model approved: Single Choice and Multiple Choice are distinct fields/controls; options may be Workflow-local or reusable Organization lists whose UI placement is deferred to UX; Process Data stores stable option identifiers and views show current labels; label changes are audited; used options are deactivated rather than deleted; Organization-list additions appear in active-instance Forms after page refresh, with no real-time update requirement.
- (decision by user) User Reference scope approved: one searchable active Member from the same Organization, optionally restricted by Teams; stable membership identifier stored and current name displayed; inactive historical selections remain visible but cannot be newly selected; field supports conditions and dynamic Task assignment, which revalidates active and Team eligibility at activation.
- (decision by user) Process Field reuse accepted: Workflow-scoped fields exist separately from Task Form controls; the Designer simply creates a field or reuses an available/inactive field; later Tasks can expose the same current value or attachments without duplicate storage; hiding/removing a control preserves data, while explicit file removal clears the value and binary but retains audit metadata. Designer UX must remain simple and clean, with complexity handled automatically or progressively disclosed.
- (decision by user) Simplified File Attachment scope approved: basic Designer settings expose label/help, required state, file categories, and maximum count; advanced settings contain extensions and size limits; defaults are five files, 10 MB each, 25 MB total; private access, server type validation, malware inspection, image/PDF preview, audit, and storage cleanup are automatic platform behavior.
- (decision by user) Data Table MVP approved: reusable row/column Process Field with required/min/max rows, typed validated columns, row operations and audit, same-row visual calculations and standard aggregates; table-level conditional visibility/enabled/required, whole-column conditional visibility, and per-row cell enabled/required rules; individual cells/rows cannot be hidden; hidden-column and disabled cells skip required validation; conditional colors and other advanced table capabilities are deferred.
- (decision by user) Calculated Field scope approved: separate read-only field with typed results and visual no-code formulas; numeric/text/date operations and table aggregates; server recalculation and publish-time dependency/cycle validation; empty sources yield empty results; runtime errors yield null rather than stale/NaN/Infinity, allow draft source preservation with error state, and block completion/routing when required; conditional formulas and scripts deferred.
- (decision by user) Conditional calculations are restored to MVP through one pedagogical visual IF/THEN/ELSE engine shared by Form-property rules, Calculated Fields, and Conditional Routing. UI uses localized natural-language Field/Operator/Value sentence blocks with examples, ordered first-match IF/ELSE IF branches and deterministic ELSE/default, preview/testing, typed validation, no nested branches, scripts, or raw expressions; app translates rules to a safe internal representation.
- (decision by user) Form layout and validation decisions: responsive twelve-column grid with per-control Auto widths favoring two standard or four compact controls per desktop row, Designer overrides through friendly width options, and up to six Compact controls; every data control has a default and optional Task-specific label with automatic accessible placement; cross-field visual validation rules can block completion/routing with a message while allowing Save Draft.
- (decision by user) Layout controls approved: non-data Section, Heading, Instruction Text, and Divider components; Sections group controls without nesting, support conditional state and semantic emphasis; visual system controls typography/colors; hidden controls collapse their grid space and following controls reflow in order without changing widths or crossing Section boundaries.
- (decision by user) Single-completion-button model approved: editable Tasks provide Save Draft and one Complete Task action with optional Task-specific label; business decisions are Process Fields; completion validates/calculates/persists/locks then routes; failure leaves Task open without duplicate downstream work; multiple custom outcome and fixed approval buttons are deferred.
- (decision by user) Clarified shared visual rules: only the pedagogical logical IF condition builder is common. Results are context-specific—Form property state, typed calculation result, validation block/message, or Conditional Routing path to an active Workflow element. Routing performs no value operation or Process Data mutation.
- (decision by user) Conditional Routing elements may chain to other Conditional Routing elements to split complex logic visually. Routing targets may therefore be active Tasks, End, or another Conditional Routing element; publication must reject automatic condition-only cycles and require every automatic chain to terminate at a Task or End.
- (decision by user) Workflow Designer summary approved and persisted: validated Start/Task/Conditional Routing/End graph with chained conditions and Task-bounded cycles; automatic cleanup and historical preservation for inactive Tasks; immutable published versions with live compatible continuation and distinct loop occurrences; one shared attributed draft per Workflow; exclusive edit lease with read-only observers, autosave, 10-minute stale expiry, Owner/Admin takeover, stale-write rejection, and configuration audit; Designer-defined Instance States set by Start/Transition and full Task occurrence transactional audit.
- (decision by user) Open Workflow draft views use lightweight status polling about every 30 seconds: revision changes show Reload rather than silently mutating the canvas; publish redirects to the immutable version; discard closes the draft view; lease release/takeover updates access and stale saves remain rejected; users without Moviqo open receive current status on next load; WebSocket-style live collaboration remains deferred.
- (decision by user) Runtime lifecycle approved: system Instance statuses Active, Completed at End, Cancelled, and recoverable Needs Attention remain separate from Designer business states; Task statuses Available, Assigned, In Progress after saved work, Needs Reassignment, Completed, and Cancelled; Owners/Admins may cancel active/attention instances with required reason, atomically cancelling open Tasks and auditing while preserving data; Completed/Cancelled cannot reopen in MVP.
- (decision by user) My Work dashboard approved with two tabs plus initiation: My Tasks opens by default and contains only actionable direct/claimed/eligible-Team Tasks with no completed history; My Processes is optional tracking with one row per started/participated Process, active default and closed filter, limited status/timeline and no access to others' Task Forms; Start a Process lists authorized Workflows; Owners/Admins receive Needs Attention and All Processes operational views.
- (decision by user) Dashboard presentation approved: fixed role-appropriate columns for My Tasks, My Processes, Needs Attention, and All Processes; searchable Start a Process card catalog; approved per-view search, filters, sortable columns and defaults, visible filter chips/clear, authorized server-side querying, pagination, and responsive mobile cards; saved views, custom columns, exports, and multi-column custom sorting deferred.
- (decision by user) Notification model: each Task has Email users when assigned or available, disabled by default and explicitly enabled per Task; it covers individual, Team availability, and administrative reassignment events while My Tasks remains authoritative. Transition business email is separately optional/default-off; overlapping recipients get one consolidated message; email excludes Process Field values/files, is localized, tracked, and retried idempotently; WhatsApp/SMS/push/in-app center deferred.
- (decision by user) Confirmed assignment email is disabled by default for both individual and Team Task assignments; Team availability email is sent only when that Task opts in. Needs Reassignment always appears in Needs Attention; separate Organization operational-problem email is approved, disabled by default, and emails Owners/Admins only when enabled.
- (decision by user) Audit model approved: immutable tenant-isolated Configuration and Transactional audits; Owners/Admins organization-wide, Designers configuration-only for authorized Workflows, Members simplified timeline only. My Processes detail shows header and authorized chronology; own Task occurrences can expand to submitted values/files, others remain progress summaries; loops are separate occurrences; admin can open full audit; audit supports search/filter/export, value-change evidence, metadata-only file evidence, timezone display, secret exclusion, and technical-log separation.
- (decision by user) Current MVP retention baseline approved as revisable later: individual Processes are retained, explicit file removal deletes binary with metadata audit, active Organizations retain business data without inactivity deletion, Owners can request secure complete export and closure, closure has 30-day recovery then production deletion with backup expiry, and beta terms assign customer data responsibility while prohibiting highly sensitive/regulated data.
- (decision by user) Security requirements added: NIST-aligned password-only authentication uses a 15-character minimum and 128-character maximum, permits passphrases, blocks common or compromised passwords, avoids composition and routine-expiration rules, supports password managers, and securely hashes credentials. User deactivation commits server-side session revocation; every protected request revalidates the session and active Organization Membership, so no WebSocket or real-time channel is required. User-facing authentication, authorization, validation, and unexpected-error responses must not expose sensitive data, account or cross-tenant resource existence, or internal diagnostics; protected logs use correlation identifiers and redact credentials and private links.
- (decision by user) Approved tenant isolation as a public-beta release boundary: every tenant-owned record and operation has one immutable Organization context; protected server operations derive and validate that context from active Membership and enforce all resource permissions; missing or mismatched context denies by default; identifier tampering cannot disclose cross-Organization existence or data; Owner and Administrator authority remains Organization-local; automated cross-Organization coverage spans all resource and operation types, and any failed or incomplete isolation suite blocks release and production deployment.
- (decision by user) Approved MVP authentication as verified email plus password. New users and self-registering initial Owners must verify email before protected Organization access or business-data use; invitation acceptance verifies only the invited address; verification and invitation links are time-limited and single-use; email changes require verification and resend operations are rate-limited. MFA is explicitly excluded from the MVP, together with enterprise SSO, identity federation, social sign-in, passwordless authentication, and passkeys.
- (decision by user) Approved private-file and Organization-export security: storage is private; each preview/download is server-authorized; stable links are non-authoritative; read-only single-file temporary storage access expires within 15 minutes; successful removal immediately revokes application access and queues binary deletion; copied application links fail after permission loss. Owner-requested exports remain Organization-scoped, are downloadable only by an active Owner, expire and are deleted within 24 hours, and audit request through deletion. Export-ready email requires authentication and contains no public storage link or business data. Moviqo cannot revoke copies already downloaded outside its control.
- (direction by user) Security design must take the current OWASP Top 10 and MITRE ATT&CK attack knowledge into account without overexpanding the MVP. Approved expert-led baseline now includes provider-managed TLS and encryption at rest, no custom cryptography, server-only and source-isolated secrets, development/production separation, no customer data in non-production, Process Data exclusion from diagnostics, and fail-safe security configuration checks. OWASP Top 10:2025 drives risk coverage, applicable OWASP ASVS 5.0.0 Level 1 requirements drive verification, and MITRE ATT&CK Enterprise SaaS scenarios drive threat modeling. Maintain requirement/control/test traceability, treat frameworks as guidance rather than certification, and record deferred controls such as MFA as accepted risks with compensating controls.
- (decision by user) Approved minimal public-beta security release gates and backup baseline. Isolation, identity/authorization, file/export, audit, OWASP ASVS Level 1, threat-model, production-configuration, dependency-vulnerability, and secret checks must pass with retained evidence; unresolved Critical/High findings block release and relevant Medium findings also block. Back up the production database and attachments every 24 hours to an encrypted destination outside the primary production failure boundary, retain 7 daily and 4 weekly copies, target 24-hour RPO and RTO, alert on backup failures, and complete an isolated restoration test before first real-data onboarding, quarterly, and after material storage changes. Organization exports are not backups; real-data onboarding is blocked until secure backup and initial restore verification operate.
- (decision by user) The MVP includes a public bilingual Moviqo landing page for beta acquisition, with Spanish default, an honest SME-focused value proposition, supported-use-case explanations, mock product visuals, security/beta information, a prominent Start Free Beta link to app registration, and Sign In link to app authentication. Use realistic but clearly labeled fictional organizations and data for purchase review, document review, and service/maintenance request examples; never fabricate real-client status, testimonials, logos, metrics, certifications, or endorsements. Landing content must remain accurate to MVP scope, responsive, accessible, lightweight, privacy-safe in analytics, linked to beta/privacy/prohibited-data terms, and isolated from protected application data.
- (override by user) For the MVP, one user account belongs to exactly one Organization through one Membership. A normalized email identifies one account and cannot be reused for another Organization; a person needing access to another Organization must register with a different email. Deactivation preserves the original Organization association, and the MVP has no Organization switcher or cross-Organization account view. Multi-Organization Memberships are deferred and this decision supersedes the PADR multi-Organization identity model.
- (decision by user) Approved Organization and user onboarding: Start Free Beta collects owner name, Organization name, unique email, password, preferred language, and confirmed timezone; requires versioned beta-terms/privacy/prohibited-data acceptance; and activates the Organization plus inherited Owner capabilities after email verification and release readiness. Owners and Administrators create Organization-bound Pending users with Member, Designer, or Administrator access; a single-use activation link expires after 7 days; the user chooses their own password and accepts terms. Pending users have no protected access. Activation can be resent or revoked. Administrators never handle user passwords and cannot manage ownership; only an Owner can transfer ownership to another active same-Organization user. All identity lifecycle actions are audited.
- (decision by user) Approved automatic beta admission with no Moviqo-operator review, an initial configurable hard limit of 10 Active Organizations, and absolute priority for currently Active Organizations. New activation or restoration succeeds only below capacity and never displaces an Active Organization or exceeds the limit. Approved initial per-Organization beta limits of 10 Pending/Active users, 5 published Workflow definitions, 200 Process starts and 500 system emails per activation-anniversary monthly cycle, and 100 MB active attachment storage, with 80% warnings, non-destructive enforcement, no automatic charges, and audited exceptions. Approved abandoned-Organization lifecycle: activity is user Sign In or authenticated product use; warn after 7 and 12 inactive days; automatically mark Dormant and release the active place after 14 days; preserve data and allow Owner-only restricted export/restoration access for at most 14 more days; restoration is first-available-capacity only and has no priority; permanently delete production records/files after 28 continuous inactive days; disclose and audit the policy. This is the approved exception to the previous no-inactivity-deletion rule.
- (decision by user) Approved final Organization deletion to remove the Owner, every other Organization-only user account and Membership, credentials, tenant configuration, business data, files, and tenant audit, after which former emails may register as entirely new identities with no relationship to deleted data. Preserve only a separate operator-only Historical Organization Register for deletion evidence and anonymous beta metrics: opaque receipt/former Organization ID, lifecycle timestamps and reason/policy, accepted terms version, aggregate user/workflow/completed-process/storage counts, and backup-expiration status. It must exclude Organization and user names, emails, IPs, credentials, workflow/task/field/file/message content, cannot be searched by email, cannot restore an Organization, and is disclosed in beta/privacy terms.
- (decision by user) Approved retaining each minimal Historical Organization Register record for no more than 24 months after final deletion. After expiry, delete the individual record; Moviqo may retain only consolidated irreversible product statistics that cannot identify, isolate, reconstruct, or link to a former Organization, user, email, Workflow, or Process.
- (decision by user) Approved localization only for Moviqo-owned application content in Spanish and English, Spanish default/fallback, with per-user interface language and Organization default for new users. Designer-authored Workflow, Task, Form, field, choice, state, validation, label, instruction, and notification content remains exactly as configured and has no MVP localization. Approved Organization-wide regional format and timezone for consistent shared dates, Date-Time values, numbers, emails, and audits, with locale-neutral storage. Owner selects Organization default currency; selecting Currency field/column automatically uses it. An advanced Use a different currency option permits exceptions. Organization default currency locks when the first Currency field/column is published; each published field currency is immutable. Block calculations/comparisons/aggregates across differing currencies; no exchange conversion; show ISO code when symbols are ambiguous. Spanish/English application translation completeness is a beta release gate.
- (decision by user) Approved Organization-wide Workflow catalog access for Designers/Admins/Owners without production Process Data access; unique active names; blank creation with Start/End; lifecycle/validation metadata, active-Process count, search/filter/sort; draft metadata updates and version-preserved names. Never-published/no-runtime Workflows may be deleted; published Workflows only archived, which blocks new starts but preserves and permits existing work; reactivation validates readiness and may require a new publication. Approved definition-only duplication with new identities, reused Organization Choice Lists, normal validation, source audit, and no runtime/history copy. Version History shows publication metadata; restoring requires reason, comparison, and creates the next sequential version. Beta limit counts 5 non-archived published definitions, archive frees a place, and draft/duplication creation remains available while publication/reactivation obeys capacity.
- (override by user) Increased initial beta capacity from 10 to 20 Active Organizations and from 5 to 10 non-archived published Workflow definitions per Organization to obtain more beta validation data. Existing minimum success criteria remain minimum outcomes rather than capacity targets. If actual use exceeds free-tier capacity, infrastructure payment and scaling will be evaluated later as a beta operating decision rather than reducing these approved product limits now.
- (decision by user) Approved beta-sized quality requirements: approximately 3-second landing/dashboard/Form usability, 2-second p95 core operations excluding files/email, representative 50-element Workflows/50-control Forms/100-row tables/1000-record dashboards, and load verification for 20 Organizations/50 concurrent users. Support current and previous Chrome/Edge/Firefox/Safari; operational mobile flows but laptop/desktop authoring at 1280x720, no native/mobile authoring. Use WCAG 2.2 A/AA as accessibility baseline without claiming formal conformance before full automated/manual verification. No uptime SLA; disclose limitations; monitor frontend/API/database/storage/email/backups/inactivity scheduling with safe health endpoints and alerts. Core business actions prevent duplicate retry outcomes and apply required related state changes all-or-nothing. Notification delivery failure never reverses successful completion/routing/assignment/state; it is recorded and retried independently, and no automatic Task-completed email was added.
- (decision by user) Approved live-version concurrency without periodic runtime Form polling or WebSockets. Current assignment and submitted Form-definition revision are checked only on explicit Save Draft, Complete Task, refresh, or reopen. If assignment changed, reject, persist none of that attempt, show reassignment message, and exit the stale Form; earlier successful saves remain. If a newer applicable Form version exists, reject the stale submission without persistence and require reload, after which all newest fields/rules/validations apply. Publishing assignment changes previews and resolves every affected open Task; known unresolvable assignments block publication; valid ones reassign access/data/status atomically and use existing optional assignment email. Server serializes publication against conflicting Task writes: publication-first rejects stale work; Task-write-first completes against the then-current single version while publication waits, then the new version applies from the resulting execution point. Never mix versions within one completion, and audit outcomes without rejected values.
- (decision by user) Approved informational Task Form model and publication validation: static guidance uses non-data Instruction Text; existing Process Fields configured read-only render as type-appropriate Display Values rather than disabled inputs; empty values show a neutral localized placeholder; explicit acknowledgment is optional through a required Yes/No field. Every active Task has one Form with meaningful visible content, while decorative-only Forms and invalid fields, lists, tables, constraints, references, rules, calculations, or dependencies block publication. Potential all-hidden states warn; invalid drafts remain saveable.
- (decision by user) Approved adding the public landing page to Gate 1 for stakeholder presentation. Gate 1 is a stakeholder-demonstrable authenticated vertical slice using synthetic data: the flow begins on the bilingual responsive landing page, continues through registration or sign-in, Organization setup, Workflow and Form design/publication, Process execution and data persistence, and ends with a completed Process plus operational views/history. Gate 2 remains the real-business-data and public-beta readiness gate.
- (decision by user) Approved formal MVP public-beta success criteria: at least five participating Organizations within the twenty-Active-Organization capacity; twenty distinct published Workflow definitions excluding versions; one hundred Processes completed by reaching End; two of the initial five Organizations active on their thirtieth day through sign-in plus meaningful product activity; simple first Workflow publication in no more than sixty minutes with thirty minutes as the target measured from Create Workflow to successful publication; at least one Organization completes the end-to-end flow without developer intervention; at least one Organization records willingness to consider an affordable paid plan; and no confirmed cross-Organization exposure or data loss unrecovered beyond approved objectives.
- (decision by user) Approved final gate distinction: Gate 1 is a deployed, feature-complete internal beta/UAT for authorized stakeholders and company manual testers, with persistent synthetic data and multiple test Organizations. Tenant isolation itself is implemented and server-enforced in Gate 1 across all Organization-scoped resources; separate test accounts can create and use multiple mutually isolated Organizations, while initial automated and manual isolation tests run there. Gate 2 does not build missing core business functionality; it resolves defects, completes production safeguards and integrations, and produces the comprehensive isolation, security, backup, restoration, monitoring, and release evidence required before deploying or promoting the same product to customers with permitted real business data.
- (decision by user) Approved artifact authority and reconciliation: the PRD and its append-only memlog are Moviqo's source of truth. The Product Brief and original PADR are starting artifacts retained for product context and historical rationale, not co-equal implementation specifications. The Product Brief is updated to current PRD decisions and marked as source context superseded by the PRD. The original PADR PDF remains unchanged as historical evidence, while a PADR supersession addendum records every material replaced or refined decision. Downstream UX, architecture, epics, stories, tests, and implementation must follow the PRD when artifacts differ.
- (decision by user) Approved final PRD defaults and readiness: initial support uses an environment-configurable email address with no live chat, ticket portal, or formal support SLA; Choice controls become searchable when more than ten active options exist; Short Text defaults to and is capped at 255 characters; Long Text defaults to and is capped at 10,000 characters. The PRD passed final business-analysis review and is ready for UX and architecture.
