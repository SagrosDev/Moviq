- (decision) Source hierarchy confirmed: prd.md is the authoritative product specification; the Product Brief informs context; padr-supersession.md identifies historical decisions and superseded PADR positions. All three sources inform UX, with PRD precedence.
- (decision) Product context confirmed: MVP is a responsive web product. Primary audience is cost-sensitive small and medium-sized businesses that lack budget to build custom applications for everyday operations. Moviqo should enable short-time process automation, with hiring offered as an example candidate workflow.
- (decision) UX north star confirmed: Moviqo must be easy and intuitive for people without development experience; inclusivity is judged by whether a non-technical family member, exemplified by the user's mother, could create a process. This reinforces the PRD target of first successful publication within thirty minutes, and no more than sixty minutes for beta success.
- (decision) Candidate hiring journey captured: a hiring-process owner or designated Designer creates the job profile, tasks, and requirements; publishes the offer; records each candidate's CV and data; conducts interviews; records interview data; and selects the person who is hired. The process is complete when the candidate is selected and hired. Candidate self-entry into Moviqo was proposed as a potentially better experience, but remains an open scope question because the PRD excludes public/anonymous initiation and currently supports authenticated Organization Members only.
- (decision) Workflow design principle confirmed: companies may model the same business process differently according to their way of working. Hiring is an example, not a fixed template. Candidate participation should be configurable by the process Designer; MVP constraints still apply to any external-access pattern.
- (decision) Access principle confirmed: an Organization Member should at minimum access the Tasks assigned directly to them or made available through an eligible Team; Members must not see other users' Tasks or unrelated Process Data. Broader instance and administrative visibility is role- and participation-dependent per the PRD.
- (decision) Primary persona confirmed: an Owner/recruiter who designs and runs their own process. Guided onboarding is a core UX requirement: Moviqo should teach the user progressively and lead them step by step while they create their first workflow, without assuming development or workflow-modeling knowledge.
- (event) Discovery distilled into working DESIGN.md and EXPERIENCE.md drafts. Visual tokens remain provisional assumptions pending review; candidate self-entry remains a future-scope extension point under current MVP access rules.
- (event) UX validation completed with rubric and accessibility lenses. Findings were resolved or logged as low-severity implementation follow-ups. DESIGN.md and EXPERIENCE.md finalized.
